About

I make complexity feel manageable.

Structure first. Polish with purpose.

I’m Alejandro, a product designer focused on digital products, e-commerce, and connected systems. I like projects where the interface is carrying real complexity: many choices, different users, or information spread across too many places.

My process moves from evidence to framing, from framing to flows, and from flows to a visual system that helps the product explain itself.

I focus onProduct strategy, UX, interface design, design systems
I bringSystems thinking, clear communication, distinctive visual direction
I care aboutTrust, usefulness, accessibility, and buildable decisions

Working principle

The interface should reveal the product’s logic.

A strong design helps people understand where they are, what changed, and what they can do next—without needing a tutorial.
